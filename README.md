## What is maimai?
maimai is a japanese arcade rhythm game created by SEGA. It's cabinet design is nearly reminiscent of a fat washing machine and it features a circular play field with an 8 button ring, a touch screen, and a 2nd display above the playing field which shows details of a player, their performance, the current song and live statistics of their gameplay.

[maimai でらっくす INTL website](https://maimai.sega.com/)
## Gameplay
It's gameplay features various notes, such as tap notes, hold notes, long notes, slider notes, touch notes, and the touch hold note. The tap and hold notes can be hit with either the buttons while the slider notes could be either but mainly uses the touchscreen to trace the notes. The touch notes exclusively uses the touchscreen. Its mechanic is usually hitting the tap and hold notes as soon as its around the displayed ring in the screen, and tapping the tap notes on the screen along with the rhythm of the song.

## Why DX/Deluxe/でらっくす？？
Before the DX series came the classic maimai games. the final version for the classic series is maimai FiNALE. The motivation of SEGA for DX is to move away from its aging hardware, Shift their game's development from their proprietary teaGFX engine to Unity to align with their Gekichumai ecosystem and reduce burden due to differing codebases, and to redesign maimai for better Quality-of-Life changes in both the Cabinet design and the overall UI/UX and game design. It also introduced a new scoring system called DX Score as maimai classic's scoring system has been topped by multiple players, which made the classic version stuck on a dead end. Finally it also introduced new mechanics which classic maimai didn't have such as Touch Notes, Touch Hold Notes, Ex Notes which always grants a Critical Perfect no matter what and the letting go of the hold notes early doesn't count as an instant miss.
